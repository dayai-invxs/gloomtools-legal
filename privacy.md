**GloomTools**

Effective date: August 21, 2026

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: consult.dayai@gmail.com

## Summary

GloomTools is an offline tool for playing Gloomhaven at the table. It has no
accounts, no servers, and no network code of any kind. It collects nothing
about you, sends nothing anywhere, and shows no advertising. Everything you
enter stays in the app's own storage on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no
advertising SDK in the app. The app makes no network requests, so there is no
server for your information to reach even in principle.

## What the app stores on your device

The app saves a single file inside its own container so your characters are
still there next time you open it. That file holds:

- Character names you type in, and the class you pick for each
- Level, which perks you have checked, and any manual deck edits
- Current health, the scenario's experience tally, and the bless, curse, and
  scenario −1 cards in play
- The cards drawn so far this scenario

This is app data, not an account. It never leaves the device except as
described under Backups below.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, sign-in, or password
- No network requests, so no data is transmitted anywhere
- No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites
- No analytics or usage telemetry
- No location data
- No contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
- No health data
- No push notifications

The app requests no permissions at all. If iOS ever prompts you for one on
behalf of GloomTools, something is wrong — please tell us.

## Backups

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups turned on, iOS includes
the app's saved file in that backup, the same as it does for other apps. That
is handled entirely by Apple under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and we have no
access to it. Turning off backups for GloomTools in iOS Settings stops this.

## Sharing

There is nothing to share. We do not sell, rent, trade, or transmit your
information, because we never receive it in the first place.

## Your choices

**Delete a character.** Swipe a row on the Character tab and choose Delete. The
character and its deck are removed immediately.

**Delete everything.** Delete the app from your device. iOS removes its
container, and with it every character and all saved state. There is no copy
anywhere else.

## Data retention

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed, and is gone
when you delete the app. We hold no copy and therefore retain nothing.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. It collects no information from
anyone, so it collects none from children either.
