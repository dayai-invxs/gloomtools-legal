**GloomTools**

Effective date: September 13, 2026

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: consult.dayai@gmail.com

## Summary

GloomTools is a tool for playing Gloomhaven at the table. It has no accounts,
no servers, and no internet features. It collects nothing about you and shows
no advertising. We, the developer, never receive anything you enter.

If you start or join a party, your device shares your character and the monster
board directly with the other devices at your table, over the local network.
That is the only data that leaves your device, and it goes only to those
devices — never to us or to any server.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no
advertising SDK in the app. The app never contacts the internet, and there is
no server operated by us or anyone else for your information to reach.

## What the app stores on your device

The app saves its data inside its own container so it is still there next time
you open it:

- **Your characters**: the names you type in, class, level, checked perks,
  manual deck edits, owned and equipped items, current health, the scenario's
  experience tally, the bless, curse, and scenario −1 cards in play, and the
  cards drawn so far this scenario
- **The monster board**: the monsters on it, their health and conditions, the
  monsters' deck, scenario level, and party size
- **A few settings**: the dashboard zoom level, and a random identifier the app
  generates for party play (see below)

This is app data, not an account. It never leaves the device except as
described under Party play and Backups below.

## Party play

Party play is optional. The app works fully without it, and nothing is shared
unless you start a party, join one, or open the Join a Party screen.

**Permission.** The first time you use party play, iOS asks whether GloomTools
may find and connect to devices on your local network. If you decline, party
play will not work and everything else will. You can change your answer at any
time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security › Local Network.

**How it connects.** Devices talk to each other directly, using Apple's
Multipeer Connectivity over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. There is no server in between.
The connection between party members is encrypted.

**What nearby devices can see.** While you are looking for a party or are in
one, your device announces itself so other players can find it. That
announcement is not encrypted and can be seen by other devices nearby. It
contains:

- Your character's name, or "Display" if you joined as a screen (while only
  browsing for parties, the name is simply "GloomTools")
- An identifier for the party you are in
- A random identifier that the app generated for your device

The device identifier is created by GloomTools itself. It is not your device's
name, not a hardware identifier, not an advertising identifier, and not linked
to your Apple Account. It exists so party members recognise each other when
they reconnect. Deleting the app deletes it.

**Who can join.** A new device can join a party only if the person running the
monsters lets them in. Devices already in that party reconnect without asking.

**What is shared with party members.**

- Your character, in full: name, class, level, perks, deck, items, health,
  experience, and scenario cards. Other players see it on a shared dashboard.
- The monster board and the monsters' deck.
- The list of who is in the party, by character name.

**What other devices keep.** Other players' devices hold your character in
memory only while the party lasts, and do not save it; it is gone when they
leave the party or close the app. The monster board is the exception: every
device in the party saves the current board in its own storage, replacing
whatever board it had before, so the scenario survives an app restart.

**When it stops.** Leaving the party, or the host ending the scenario, stops
all sharing and all announcements.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, sign-in, or password
- No internet connections, and no servers
- No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites
- No analytics or usage telemetry
- No location data
- No contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
- No health data
- No push notifications

The only permission the app asks for is local network access, and only when
you use party play.

## Backups

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups turned on, iOS includes
the app's saved data in that backup, the same as it does for other apps. That
is handled entirely by Apple under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and we have no
access to it. Turning off backups for GloomTools in iOS Settings stops this.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or receive your information. The only sharing is
the party play described above: directly between the devices at your table,
and only when you choose to use it.

## Your choices

**Stop sharing.** Tap Leave Party on the Character tab. To stop the app using
the local network at all, turn off GloomTools under Settings › Privacy &
Security › Local Network.

**Clear the board.** On the Monsters tab, open the settings sheet and choose
Clear the Board.

**Delete a character.** Swipe a row on the Character tab and choose Delete. The
character and its deck are removed immediately.

**Delete everything.** Delete the app from your device. iOS removes its
container, and with it every character, the board, the device identifier, and
all settings. We hold no copy anywhere else.

## Data retention

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed, and is gone
when you delete the app. We hold no copy and therefore retain nothing. A board
you shared in a party also stays on the other party members' devices until
they clear it, end the scenario, or delete the app.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We collect no information from
anyone, so we collect none from children either.
